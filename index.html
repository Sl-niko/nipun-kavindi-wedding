Questions

Good to know

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply quickly.

How do my guests receive the invitation?

You get a list of personal links, one per guest. Tap a guest's link and WhatsApp opens with a ready-written message, so you just press send.

Can I change details after sending?

Yes. If the time or venue changes, we update it and every guest's link shows the new details instantly. No need to resend.

How long does it take?

Template designs are ready within 24 hours of receiving your details. Fully custom designs take 3–5 days.

Can I have separate invitations for the wedding and homecoming?

Yes. We can set up two events, each with its own details and guest list. Just let us know when you order.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or card. You only pay once you've approved the preview.
